SECTION 1 — INTRODUCTION & BACKGROUND

1.1 Overview
Vantage Health Systems (VHS) is a regional healthcare network operating 6 facilities across Tennessee and Kentucky, serving approximately 1.2 million patients annually. VHS employs 3,400 staff across clinical and administrative functions.

1.2 Purpose
VHS is seeking proposals from qualified IT managed services providers to deliver comprehensive managed IT services and cybersecurity support. The selected vendor will replace our current fragmented IT support model with a unified, proactive managed services partnership.

1.3 Contract Term
Initial term: 2 years (May 1, 2025 – April 30, 2027)
Renewal option: One 1-year renewal at VHS's discretion

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SECTION 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Help Desk & End-User Support
- Tier 1, 2, and 3 help desk support covering approximately 850 endpoints (laptops, workstations, mobile devices)
- 24x7x365 availability with SLA target: P1 issues resolved within 4 hours
- On-site support at Nashville headquarters (1 FTE minimum on-site)
- Remote support for remaining 5 facilities

2.2 Network Monitoring & Management
- 24x7 network monitoring across all 6 sites
- Network infrastructure management (switching, routing, wireless)
- Monthly network performance reporting
- Incident escalation and resolution

2.3 Endpoint Security & Management
- Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR) deployment and management for all 850 endpoints
- Patch management — OS and third-party applications
- Mobile Device Management (MDM)
- Email security (anti-phishing, anti-malware)

2.4 Cybersecurity Services
- Security Information & Event Management (SIEM) monitoring
- Quarterly vulnerability assessments
- Annual penetration testing (internal and external)
- Phishing simulation training program
- Incident response planning and tabletop exercises

2.5 HIPAA Compliance Support
- Ongoing HIPAA risk assessment support
- Policy and procedure review
- Staff training support
- Breach notification advisory

2.6 Reporting & Governance
- Monthly executive dashboard
- Quarterly business reviews (QBR) with VHS leadership
- SLA performance reporting

SECTION 3 — VENDOR QUALIFICATIONS

3.1 Mandatory Requirements
Vendors must meet ALL of the following to be considered:

[ ] Minimum 5 years of experience delivering managed IT services
[ ] Healthcare industry experience (minimum 3 healthcare clients with references)
[ ] HIPAA business associate agreement (BAA) execution capability
[ ] SOC 2 Type II certification (current, within 12 months)
[ ] Cyber liability insurance: minimum $2M per occurrence / $5M aggregate
[ ] General liability insurance: minimum $1M per occurrence
[ ] 24x7 NOC operations capability (own or contracted)

3.2 Preferred Qualifications
- ISO 27001 certification
- HITRUST CSF certification or experience
- Experience with Epic EHR system support
- Existing team in Tennessee or ability to place on-site staff within 30 days
